Personal-disc setup reproduced from a fresh workspace

Date: 6 November 2026. Project: the separate original GPL god-hand-pc tooling, not new Rust work.

Why another build

The earlier tested native executable used a manually prepared research checkout. It does not prove another owner can reproduce the full setup. The new command fetches the pinned upstream and eight exact dependency revisions into a distinct ignored workspace, verifies dependency license receipts and the complete personal USA ISO, then runs the boot and 81-overlay generation/build pipeline.

The existing evaluated checkout, its working executable, original screenshots, prototype experiment and paused Rust edits are not modified. All game-derived outputs stay private. Only original orchestration, tests, dependency metadata and instructions are public.

Scope and remaining checks
